Scientific Background

Studies show that vaping for just 30 days can reduce lung function by up to 40% in some individuals. The nicotine and other chemicals in vapes cause inflammation, reduce cilia function (the hair-like structures that clear lungs), and damage alveoli where oxygen exchange happens.

Athletes who vape experience decreased VO2 max (maximum oxygen consumption), reduced endurance, slower recovery times, and increased risk of respiratory illnesses.
